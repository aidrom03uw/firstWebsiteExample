<h1>Campus Study Space Guide</h1>

<h2>Finding a Good Place to Study</h2>


<p>Students need different kinds of study spaces depending on whether they are working alone, meeting with a group, or attending an online meeting. </p>


<p>This guide introduces several study spaces and explains their main features.</p>


<p>

Some spaces are designed for <strong>quiet individual study</strong>,

while others are better for <em>group collaboration</em>.

</p>


<section>

     <h2>Quiet Study Spaces</h2>

     <p>

         Quiet study spaces are useful when students need to read,

         write, or concentrate without interruption.

     </p>

</section>


<section>

     <h2>Group Study Spaces</h2>

     <p>

         Group study spaces allow students to discuss projects

         and work together without disturbing other students.

     </p>

</section>


<h2>What to Look For</h2>

<ul>

     <li>Available seating</li>

     <li>Power outlets</li>

     <li>Reliable Wi-Fi</li>

     <li>Low noise level</li>

</ul>


<h2>How to Choose a Study Space</h2>

<ol>

     <li>Decide whether you need individual or group space</li>

     <li>Check the expected noise level</li>

     <li>Check whether power outlets are available</li>

     <li>Select the space that best fits your needs</li>

</ol>
